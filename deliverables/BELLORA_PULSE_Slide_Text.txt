BELLORA.PULSE THE PROBLEM BELLORA.PULSE / Project presentation 03 / 16 Why BELLORA.PULSE? Following AI often involves several separate searches. Scattered updates Too many starting points News and announcements are spread across publishers and websites. Repeated stories The same event appears again Different headlines can describe one announcement or development. Separate discovery Learning takes extra effort Useful tools, podcasts and expert voices live on different platforms. The problem to solve: help people find and organise relevant information.
BELLORA.PULSE WHAT WE WANT TO ACHIEVE BELLORA.PULSE / Project presentation 04 / 16 Vision & Objective Make the AI ecosystem easier to explore.
